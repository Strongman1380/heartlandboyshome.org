Community Service

Our residents give back — building character, responsibility, and community ties through meaningful service work.

Giving Back as Part of Growing Up

At Heartland Boys Home, community service is a cornerstone of our program. We believe that contributing to something bigger than yourself is one of the most powerful ways to develop responsibility, empathy, and pride.

Our residents regularly participate in service projects throughout the Geneva area and surrounding communities — from yard work and clean-up projects to helping local organizations with tasks that need an extra set of hands. It's good for the community, and it's good for the boys.

What Our Residents Can Help With

Yard & Grounds Work

Mowing, raking, weeding, landscaping, and general outdoor upkeep for homes, churches, or community spaces.

Clean-Up Projects

Litter clean-up, park maintenance, painting, and beautification projects around town.

Assisting Local Organizations

Helping food pantries, churches, shelters, or other nonprofits with tasks, events, or day-to-day needs.

Light Construction & Repairs

Basic maintenance, painting, moving materials, or assisting with building and repair projects.

Seasonal Work

Snow removal, fall clean-up, garden prep, and other seasonal tasks for community members in need.

Community Events

Setup, teardown, and support for local events, festivals, and community gatherings.
